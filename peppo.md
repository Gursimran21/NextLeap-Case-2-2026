# Case 2 : Peppo

## Solution to Peppo's Mapping Outcomes to Opportunities & User Research: Cracking Growth at Peppo: Segmenting for Scale

To crack growth at Peppo, product manager Aditi Rao must transition Peppo from broadcast marketing to targeted behavioral and transactional segmentation. Below is the comprehensive segmentation model, user research plan, data interpretation, and strategic recommendations.

### Segmentation Model

To generate actionable insights, Peppo’s user base should be categorized using three primary axes:

| Axis          | Metric / Attributes | Actionability & Implementation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Monetization & Frequency (RFM)**  | Bronze (1–2 orders, ₹250 AOV, 12% ret.), Silver (3–6 orders, ₹310 AOV, 29% ret.), Gold (>6 orders, ₹420 AOV, 54% ret.)        | **High / Easy:** Gold users are 4.5x more retained than Bronze. Easy to track via order history and target with retention perks. |
| **Acquisition Source (Intent Level)**  | Instagram Ads (7% W4 ret., ₹420 CAC), SEO (16% W4 ret., ₹90 CAC), Referrals (20% W4 ret., ₹0 CAC), Blogger Collabs (12% W4 ret., ₹250 CAC)        | **High / Easy:** Highlights low-intent acquisition. Blended cost per W4 retained user on Instagram is ₹6,000 vs. ₹562.50 for SEO. |
| **Product Engagement Patterns** | Recipe Savers (Avg. 2.03 orders), Video Viewers (Avg. 1.99 orders), High-AOV Single Users vs. Family Meal Buyers | **Medium / Needs Research:** Recipe saving correlates with slightly higher order frequencies. Requires research to tie features to core user motivation. |

### User Research Plan

**1. Quantitative Research** 

- **Funnel Drop-Off & Feature Co-relation:** Analyze drop-offs from app download $\rightarrow$ first recipe view $\rightarrow$ checkout $\rightarrow$ 2nd order. Measure whether video viewing correlates with higher first-order completion.

- **NPS Deep Dive:** Trigger NPS surveys at key moments (e.g., after order 1, order 3, and order 6) segmenting scores across Gold, Silver, and Bronze tiers.

**2. Qualitative Interviews**

- **Target Cohorts:** Interview 15 **Gold users** (high-intent, repeat buyers) and 15 **Bronze churned users** (dropped off after 1 order).  

- **Key Questions:**
  
  - **Gold Users:** "What specific habit or routine does Peppo replace for you?" "What made you move from 1–2 orders to cooking regularly with Peppo?"
  - **Bronze/Churned Users:** "What was the main reason you didn't order a second time?" "Was it meal prep effort, portion size, price, or recipe options?"
  - **Hypotheses to Validate:** Users churn primarily due to recipe fatigue/limited variety (62%) and price sensitivity (31%), rather than cooking difficulty.
